# Rooftop Level Production Brief

Scene: `rooftop` / `黄金大师天台局`

Status: playable Phaser level with unified v9 rooftop progress rasters and six embedded clue hotspots. Future edits must preserve the casual rooftop bragging premise and the calibrated embedded-object clue design.

## Theme

Gold-buying crowd psychology: a rooftop gossip session turns price alerts, receipts, group chats, and leverage documents into social pressure.

## Scene Logic

People are on the rooftop to smoke, drink, look at phones, boast, and trade rumors after work. They are not doing formal office work. The protagonist belongs at the edge of the group as a skeptical smoker, not pasted into the center or standing on pipes.

## Clue And Difficulty Plan

This is level 2 and must be harder than the office:

- 6 clues, increasing from 5.
- Clues are embedded across wall, hand-held phone, AC poster, receipt, phone alert, and contract/key objects.
- Do not scatter loose props across an empty floor.
- Every true clue needs nearby same-material decoys, such as ordinary papers, phones, receipts, cups, and rooftop hardware.
- Semantics must be direct: contract/key/folder for leverage, phone for group chat, receipt for gold purchase, not unrelated warning signs.

## Character State Plan

Use `rooftop-v9-progress-0` through `rooftop-v9-progress-6` or a better same-camera replacement. State changes must remain visible in the protagonist's face/body language and must not introduce cutout edges, floating limbs, or mismatched lighting.

## Rejection Gates

- no toy-scale or pasted protagonist
- no people working at rooftop desks
- no phones bigger than people
- no warning sign used as financial clue unless the scene itself makes that relation visible
- no hotspot centers guessed without source-pixel calibration
