# Office Level Production Brief

Scene: `office` / `键盘声变轻了`

Status: playable Phaser level with layered office background, independent Zhou Qiming states, and five clue interactions. Future edits must preserve the focused workplace-anxiety theme and avoid turning the level into a cross-topic sampler.

## Theme

Workplace pressure and mild wealth anxiety: Zhou Qiming is distracted at his desk because work noise, loss screenshots, and small get-rich fantasies are all competing for attention.

## Scene Logic

The scene is an open office desk. Every clue must plausibly belong in a worker's immediate environment: phone screens, chat windows, meeting pressure, lottery residue, and payment notes. Later-level subjects such as full lottery counters, rooftop gold bragging, AI launch stages, or stock heatlists must stay out of this level.

## Clue And Difficulty Plan

This is the first level, so it stays introductory:

- 5 clues only.
- Larger visible clue bodies than later levels.
- Few decoys, but no default hover spoilers.
- Each clue is a specific object or screen region, not a generic text block.
- Hit feedback stays local: K-line pulse, chat stamp, gold line scan, paper bounce, and scratch sheen.

## Character State Plan

Zhou Qiming must remain an independent scene unit with `progress-0` through `progress-5`. Every found clue must visibly change expression, posture, eye line, shoulder tension, or body language. CSS-drawn face parts and pasted background crops are forbidden.

## Rejection Gates

- no cross-level clue dumping
- no fake narrative text unsupported by art
- no hover-revealed answers
- no patchwork face or transparent avatar overlay
- no completion page that becomes a dashboard
